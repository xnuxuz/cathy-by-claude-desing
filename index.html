🌸 Kristina

My name is Kristina Angreani, born and raised in Cengkareng, Jakarta Barat 🇮🇩. I am a vocational high school graduate from Taiwan 🇹🇼 and currently work as a Mandarin Translator & Control Room Staff at IWIP (Indonesia Weda Bay Industrial Park), Maluku Utara.

Today I use Mandarin every day at work — interpreting directly between Chinese and Indonesian colleagues and operating Chinese-language systems in the control room. I am a fast learner, responsible, and adapt quickly to demanding environments. 🌿
